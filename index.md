I am an AI Scientist at [Mistral](https://mistral.ai/) in Paris, working on multimodal large language models and robotics. Previously I was a Staff Research Scientist at [Meta](https://tech.facebook.com/reality-labs/) in Seattle and Zurich, where I worked on spatial AI for AR/VR and robotics. I created and lead the [BOP benchmark](https://bop.felk.cvut.cz/), which became the standard for evaluating 6DoF object pose estimation. My work also includes methods (e.g., [FoundPose](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2311.18809), [EPOS](https://cmp.felk.cvut.cz/epos/), [LISA](http://www.iri.upc.edu/people/ecorona/lisa/paper.pdf), [NCF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2208.00113.pdf), [CNOS](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2023W/R6D/papers/Nguyen_CNOS_A_Strong_Baseline_for_CAD-Based_Novel_Object_Segmentation_ICCVW_2023_paper.pdf)) and datasets (e.g., [HOT3D](https://facebookresearch.github.io/hot3d), [T-LESS](https://cmp.felk.cvut.cz/t-less/), [BlenderProc](https://dlr-rm.github.io/BlenderProc/README_BlenderProc4BOP.html)) for pose estimation and detection of objects and hands. Beyond research, I organize the [R6D workshops](http://cmp.felk.cvut.cz/sixd/workshop_2026/) and serve as an Area Chair at major vision conferences. I received my PhD in computer vision advised by [Prof. Jiri Matas](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=EJCNY6QAAAAJ) at the [Czech Technical University in Prague](https://fel.cvut.cz/en), during which I interned at [Microsoft Research](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/lab/microsoft-research-redmond/) and [Google](http://www.stefan-hinterstoisser.com/).
